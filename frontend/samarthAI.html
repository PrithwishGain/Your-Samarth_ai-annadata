Farmer Help

Questions about your Annyadata journey?

You can always ask Samarth by voice instead of navigating through the portal.

What does Samarth do for my farm?

Samarth is your voice-first AI companion. You can ask about cultivation, crop health, weather, schemes, insurance, quality and the next step in your Annyadata journey.

Does Samarth replace a field expert?

No. Samarth provides guidance, while Annyadata's field volunteers can verify crop and farm conditions on the ground.

How does direct procurement work?

Annyadata aims to connect participating farmers more directly with procurement and rice-mill relationships, while using quality and field verification to support the process.

What should I do if I see a crop problem?

Tap the Samarth voice orb and describe what you see in your own language. Start with the crop, the symptom and when you first noticed it.
