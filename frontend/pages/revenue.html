💼 Business Model

Making Samarth sustainable.

Samarth is designed to keep essential assistance accessible while creating multiple sustainable revenue streams through premium services, partnerships and institutional adoption.

⭐

Premium Subscriptions

B2C

Users who need deeper analysis, personalized planning and higher AI usage can access advanced capabilities through a future subscription plan.

Revenue opportunity Recurring subscription revenue from users who choose advanced Samarth capabilities.
🤝

Partnership Revenue

B2B & B2C

Samarth can connect users with relevant agricultural, financial and service partners when appropriate.

Revenue opportunity Potential referral, lead-generation or distribution arrangements with eligible partners.
🏛️

Institutional Licensing

B2B

Government departments, NGOs, FPOs and other organizations can deploy Samarth for their communities and programs.

Revenue opportunity Annual platform licensing, deployment and organization specific knowledge-base services.
🏢

Enterprise & API

Enterprise

Organizations can integrate Samarth's voice and AI capabilities into their own applications or service platforms.

Revenue opportunity API usage, enterprise plans, custom integrations and technical support.
🛠️

Implementation & Support

Services

Organizations adopting Samarth may require onboarding, customization, knowledge-base setup, training and ongoing technical support.

Revenue opportunity One-time implementation fees and recurring support or maintenance contracts.
🌱

Service Marketplace

Future

In the future, Samarth could help users discover trusted local services, products and business opportunities through a carefully governed marketplace.

Revenue opportunity Potential transaction, listing or qualified-lead fees, subject to appropriate safeguards and partnerships.

A diversified revenue model

Instead of depending on a single source of income, Samarth can combine consumer, partnership and institutional models to build long-term sustainability.
